Field notes: getting to net zero

Ranked by where the gap actually is, based on what the data shows above.

01 — WINTER SUPPLY

Close the winter gap first

Nov–Feb production runs 900–1,500 kWh/month against 2,800–4,000 kWh/month of consumption. That's nearly the whole annual shortfall. About 3–5 kW more panel capacity, or a battery charged on summer surplus, would target this directly.

02 — DAYLIGHT ALIGNMENT

Nudge the last 20% into daylight

80% of sessions already happen in morning or afternoon sun. A simple time-of-use incentive for the remaining evening/overnight charges would push self-sufficiency up without any new hardware.

03 — OVERNIGHT COVERAGE

Size a small battery for the rare long overnight charge

Only 15 of 336 nights saw overnight charging, and it's almost always one continuous ~4-hour session, not repeated on/off. A modest battery sized to that pattern could cover it on stored daytime solar.

04 — TRACKING

Re-baseline every 12 months, not every month

Production swings 3–4× between summer and winter. Net-zero progress should be read on a rolling annual basis, and re-checked if EV charging frequency grows.

Method: a 15-minute interval counts as EV charging when total Consumption exceeds 10 kW; consecutive flagged intervals are grouped into one session.
